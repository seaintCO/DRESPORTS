Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this coaching for?
Coaching is designed for dedicated individuals—ranging from aspiring collegiate athletes to committed everyday performers—who are serious about optimizing their physical output, preventing injury, and adhering to science-based protocols.
Do I need access to a full gym?
While a full gym is ideal for maximizing certain strength adaptations, programs can be entirely customized to the equipment you currently have available, focusing heavily on biomechanics and intent over pure load.
Is online coaching as effective as in-person?
Yes. Through detailed video analysis, comprehensive tracking platforms, and regular communication, we maintain strict oversight of your form and progress, delivering a premium clinical-level experience remotely.
How do the virtual consultations work?
Consultations are conducted via secure video call. We discuss your athletic history, current limitations, nutritional habits, and specific goals to determine if our methodologies align and construct an initial roadmap.
How long are the programs?
Programs are typically structured in 12-week macrocycles to ensure adequate time for physiological adaptation, but coaching is delivered on a month-to-month continuous basis for long-term athletic development.
